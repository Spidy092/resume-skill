# Recruiter and hiring-manager rubric

Do not convert this review into a single universal score.

## Pass 1: 10-second scan

Without rereading, identify the apparent role/level, the three strongest qualifications in the top half, whether recent relevant experience is clear, and any space that does not improve the decision. Fail when the target is ambiguous, the strongest proof is buried, or density prevents scanning.

## Pass 2: hiring-manager evidence

For each major target requirement, identify the exact proving bullet. Evaluate technical depth, personal ownership, production context, outcome, recency, terminology, and project relevance. A skills-list mention is not proof of applied experience.

## Pass 3: skeptical verification

Flag unsupported quantities, implausible causality, title inflation, skills broader than supporting bullets, repetition, generic AI phrasing, unexplained chronology, repository/claim mismatches, and confidential details.

## Recommendation classes

- `release`: no blocking issue.
- `revise`: credible candidate, but a material comprehension or fit issue remains.
- `do_not_release`: factual, parsing, privacy, or severe positioning failure.
