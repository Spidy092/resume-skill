# Job-description keyword mapping

Keywords are evidence labels, not magic words.

## Classification

- `supported_direct`: exact skill or responsibility appears in evidence.
- `supported_alias`: an approved alias maps to evidence.
- `adjacent`: related experience supports discussion but not the exact claim.
- `unsupported`: no evidence; report as a gap and omit from claims.
- `unknown`: evidence may exist but requires a candidate answer.

Prioritize explicit requirements, repeated technologies/responsibilities/domain terms, role and seniority language, then preferred skills. Preserve exact spellings where useful and use acronym plus expanded form when the job uses both.

Put applied skills in evidence-bearing bullets. Use the skills section for discoverability, not as a substitute for proof. Never add an unsupported keyword to the resume; put it in the gap report.

The deterministic matcher is a baseline from declared skills and aliases. Review context, negation, required/preferred language, and substring false positives.
